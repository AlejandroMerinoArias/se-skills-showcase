# E-12 — Do cave lanterns appear as pure (255,235,4) pixels in the dark cave, and from what range?

## Question
Inside the dark cave, do the four cave lanterns still appear in the semantic image as pure (255,235,4) pixels, and
from what range?

Criteria:
- **Purity:** the share of near-lantern pixels (each channel within 40 of the colour, but not exact) among all
  lantern-like pixels, and the count of any other non-black pixels.
- **Range:** the camera-to-visible-centre distance (E-10 (not published)). The detection fraction is taken per 10 m bin, over the
  frames that put the centre inside the image geometrically.

## Command
Same flights as E-10 (not published):
- `tools/probes/sim_session.sh 1300 .probe_logs/e10/runK -- python3 tools/probes/E-10_flight.py …` for K = 1..3;
- analysis: `python tools/probes/E-10_analyse.py .probe_logs/e05/scene.json .probe_logs/e10/run{1,2,3}`.

The flight passes along the dark gallery, hovers 4 m from Lantern (3) in its niche, and backs away 60 m facing
it.

## Verdict
VERDICT: yes. In the dark cave, lanterns appear only as exact (255,235,4) pixels: 0 impure pixels in 329 549
lantern pixels, and nothing else in the semantic image (3 runs). Detection is 100 % of in-view frames up to 50 m
and about 70 % at 50–60 m. It falls to 0 by 90 m.

3D localisation from semantic pixels plus depth is limited to about 50 m by the depth range (49.5 m, E-01). An
exact-colour mask is therefore a complete detector: SYS-F-004 needs no threshold tuning.

## Limits
- Lantern (3) sits in a gallery niche. The tunnel lanterns (E-18) were not flown to, but they share the same
  prefab (E-10 (not published)) and the same semantic rendering.
- In-view is geometric only. Frames beyond 60 m may be occluded by the niche walls rather than too small to
  see.
- A lantern at 50 m is about 11 px tall (fx = 120, 4.5 m body).
